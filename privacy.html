Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Spend Tracker is an expense tracker made by Vasanthakumar V. Your expenses stay on your device, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync. There's no Spend Tracker account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the developer has no server that receives your data.

What's stored on your device

The app stores what you enter or import: expenses, categories, budgets, repeating expenses and settings. It also keeps recent exchange rates. Files you import are read on your device and aren't uploaded anywhere by the app.

This data isn't encrypted by the app. The optional app lock keeps people out of the app, but it doesn't encrypt the data. Your device's own storage protection still applies.

Google Drive backup and sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app copies your data to a "Spend Tracker" folder in your own Drive so that your devices can sync. Data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The developer can't see it.

The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it use files it created itself. It can't see your other Drive files. Your sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage (on Android, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore).

Spend Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is used only to provide backup and sync, and isn't sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Exchange rates

To convert currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. The request contains none of your expense data, but like any internet request it shows that service your IP address.

App lock and widgets

If you set an app lock, only a salted hash of the password is stored, and it isn't synced to Drive. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by your operating system, and the app never sees biometric data. Home-screen widgets show a summary of your spending, and can be set to hide amounts.

Optional tips

Tips in the Android app are handled by Google Play, so your payment details go to Google and aren't seen by the app. The app records the date of your first tip with your data, to show that you're a supporter. On desktop, the app can open a web page for supporting the app, which is outside the app. Tips made on Ko-fi are handled by Ko-fi under its own policies.

Android device backup

If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include this app's data in your device backup to your Google account. You control this in your device settings.

Sharing

The developer doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. Data only leaves your device in the ways described above, or when you export your expenses as a CSV file yourself.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: delete expenses in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its data from that device.
  • In Google Drive: delete the "Spend Tracker" folder from your Drive.
  • Drive access: tap Disconnect in the app, or remove the app's access in your Google Account.

Because the developer doesn't hold your data, these steps are in your hands and can't be done for you.

Children

Spend Tracker isn't directed at children under 13.
