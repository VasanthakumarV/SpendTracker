Track spending. Keep it yours.

A simple expense tracker that works offline, keeps your data on your device, and syncs through your own Google Drive. No account, no ads.

Works offline

Everything is saved on your device first. No sign-up, no network needed.

Your own backup

Optional sync through a "Spend Tracker" folder in your Google Drive. Edit on several devices; changes merge on their own.

Any currency

Every currency, each expense in its own, with totals converted using rates cached for offline use.

Budgets and forecast

Monthly limits per category, and an estimate of where the month will end.

Recurring expenses

Rent, subscriptions and bills are added for you when they're due.

Import

Bank and card statements (CSV, Excel), Google Pay and Amazon history, read on your device.

Free, with nothing locked

Every feature is free. If the app is useful to you, you can leave a tip.
