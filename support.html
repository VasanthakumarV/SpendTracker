Support the app

Spend Tracker is free, with no ads and no account, and nothing is locked. If it's useful to you, a tip helps keep it going. Thank you!

Tip from the Android app

Open the app, go to Settings → General → Support the App and pick an amount. It goes through Google Play.

Tips can only be made in the Android app, not in the desktop app.

Other ways to help

Rate the app on Google Play, tell a friend, or send feedback about what's missing or broken.
